01 ABOUT

Fine arts foundation, systems-first practice.

I'm a UX/UI designer trained at the School of Visual Arts in New York, where a fine arts background taught me to see composition, contrast, and hierarchy before I ever opened a design tool. That visual instinct now drives how I approach product problems: structure first, decoration last.

I hold Google's UX Design and AI Essentials certifications, and I build with AI tools as part of my everyday workflow — from research synthesis to rapid prototyping — not as a novelty, but as infrastructure. I'm currently looking for roles in AI product design, design technology, founding design, and fintech or enterprise UX.
